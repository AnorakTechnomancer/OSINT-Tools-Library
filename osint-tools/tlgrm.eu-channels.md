### What does tlgrm.eu channels do?

tlgrm.eu provides a searchable directory of public Telegram channels. Its channel directory can be searched using a name, description or keyword, while channels are also organised into subject categories including politics, videos and movies, entertainment, books, music, news, education etc. to name a few.

Individual channel pages can provide the channel name, username, subscriber count and channel posts. For example, the directory provides dedicated pages for channels such as Telegram News and Telegram Tips.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s a great Telegram discovery and indexing resource.

### How to Use:

**1. Go to the Channels directory and enter a relevant channel name, description term or keyword in the search field. You can also browse one of the site's subject categories.**

**2. Review the resulting channels. Record the channel name, username, description, subscriber count where displayed and the directory URL. Open the individual channel page to inspect available posts and statistics.**

**Note**: The channel discovery appears to be quite broad so unlikely to find more niche channels here.&#x20;

**3. Follow the channel's Telegram link to verify the information against the primary Telegram source. Record the retrieval date and preserve relevant posts/screenshots separately rather than treating the directory listing as the authoritative source.**

### Cookies:&#x20;

The site uses Google Analytics 4 and Yandex Metrica analytics technologies.&#x20;

Captured Yandex cookies include \_ym\_uid, which provides a pseudonymous visitor identifier, \_ym\_d, which records first-session/identifier timing, and \_ym\_isad, which detects whether an ad blocker is being used. The site also sets Google FCCDCF and FCNEC cookies associated with consent/advertising technology, and a bcom\_on cookie associated with the third-party comments service comments.app. These are analytics, consent, advertising and functionality technologies rather than Telegram authentication cookies.

### Use in Reporting

tlgrm.eu channels can be used to:

* Discover public Telegram channels associated with a subject or keyword.
* Identify channel names and @usernames.
* Locate channels that may otherwise be difficult to discover through ordinary web searches.
* Browse channels by subject category.
* Identify potentially relevant news, political, technology, business, cryptocurrency and other specialist channels.
* Establish a lead for further investigation on Telegram.
* Record subscriber counts and other displayed channel information at a particular point in time.
* Locate individual channel pages that can then be cross-checked against Telegram.
* Support the construction of a list of relevant Telegram sources.
* Provide an initial discovery layer before using more specialised Telegram search or archival tools.

As an example of real-world application, [a Pew Research Center study on alternative social media](https://www.pewresearch.org/journalism/2022/10/06/alternative-social-media-methodology/) used several Telegram channel directories, including tlgrm.eu, to construct a seed list of channels for research. The researchers subsequently collected posts from the identified channels using their own Python tooling.

| **Capabilities**                                                              | **Limitations**                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enables search of public channels by name, description or keyword.            | It’s a third-party directory rather than Telegram's own service.                                                                               |
| Provides individual pages for indexed channels.                               | Directory coverage is not overly comprehensive.                                                                                                |
| Displays channel identifiers and available subscriber information.            | A channel not appearing in the directory does not establish that it does not exist.                                                            |
| Provides access to channel posts and statistics on indexed channel pages.     | Subscriber counts and other displayed information can change over time.                                                                        |
| Allows channel owners/users to submit public channel links to the directory.  | The site states that only public t.me/username links and English-language channels are accepted for directory submissions, limiting coverage.  |

### Summary

tlgrm.eu channels is particularly useful during the early stages of an investigation when the relevant Telegram username or channel is not yet known but should be used as a channel-discovery/indexing resource rather than as a complete archive of Telegram or a definitive source for channel statistics.&#x20;

### Ownership

Third-party, independent directory not affiliated with Telegram.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat directory listings as leads, not proof.
* Verify important findings against the underlying Telegram channel.
* Do not assume a channel's name or description establishes who operates it.
* Be particularly careful with political, conflict and breaking-news channels: channel content may contain unverified claims, propaganda or recycled material.
* Preserve the original Telegram URL and relevant post identifiers when using material in a report.
* Avoid unnecessarily reproducing personal information from channel descriptions or posts.
