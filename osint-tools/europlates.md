### What does Europlates do?

Europlates is essentially a visual licence-plate reference library. It organises plate photographs by country and, where available, by categories such as regular plates, former/older plate designs, special plates, diplomatic plates, military plates etc.&#x20;

For example, the UK section contains separate categories for regular, special, diplomatic and military plates, while other countries have additional historical or regional categories.

**The lowdown:** It’s best thought of as a reference and comparison tool rather than a vehicle-registration lookup service.&#x20;

### How to Use:

**1. Open Europlates and identify the country or region you want to investigate. Browse the country index or map.**

**2. Compare the plate visible in your source image with the examples provided. Look at colour, dimensions, characters, country identifiers, EU/Eurobands and specialist markings.**&#x20;

**3. Record the likely country and plate type, then corroborate the identification using another authoritative source before including it as a fact in reporting.**&#x20;

### Cookies:&#x20;

Europlates uses analytics cookies from Google Analytics and StatCounter. \_ga, \_ga\_\* and \_gid are associated with Google Analytics and are used to distinguish visitors and analyse website usage. is\_unique, is\_visitor\_unique and sc\_is\_visitor\_unique are associated with StatCounter and are used for visitor/uniqueness tracking.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

Europlates can be particularly useful during visual OSINT investigations to:

* Identify a vehicle's likely country by comparing a plate photographed in an incident image with country-specific examples to establish a likely registration jurisdiction.
* Distinguish plate types via specialist categories which can help determine whether a plate is ordinary, diplomatic, military, governmental or another special type.
* Support geolocation as a distinctive plate design can provide an additional geographic clue when combined with road signs, architecture, landscape, language and other visual indicators.
* Investigate historical imagery as older plate categories can be useful when analysing archival photographs or footage where the registration design has subsequently changed.&#x20;
* Verify image-based claims.

**Important Note:** Europlates should normally be treated as supporting evidence, rather than definitive proof of where a vehicle was located. A vehicle's registration plate indicates a registration jurisdiction, not necessarily its current location.

| **Capabilities**                                                                           | **Limitations**                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Large visual collection of European registration plates.                                   | It is primarily a visual reference library, not a vehicle-registration database.                                          |
| Country-by-country organisation makes comparison relatively straightforward.               | It generally cannot identify the owner or registered keeper of a vehicle.                                                 |
| Includes specialist categories such as diplomatic and military plates for some countries.  | Coverage and categorisation vary between countries.                                                                       |
| Includes some former/historical plate designs, useful for older imagery.                   | Photographs may not represent every current or historical plate variation.                                                |
| Contains both site-owner photographs and submitted images.                                 | User-submitted material requires appropriate source assessment and should not automatically be treated as authoritative.  |
| Covers countries outside Europe as well as its main European collection.                   | Plate identification from a poor-quality photograph can remain uncertain even when a similar example is found.            |

### Summary

Europlates is a free visual licence-plate reference resource useful for identifying and comparing vehicle registration plates with its best fit in the collection and verification stages of the OSINT workflow.&#x20;

It is particularly valuable for image analysis, geolocation and vehicle-related investigations, but it should not be confused with a registration-number lookup or vehicle-owner database.

### Ethical Considerations

* Use Europlates primarily for legitimate research, journalism, verification and investigative purposes.
* Do not assume that identifying a plate's country identifies the vehicle owner's identity or location.
* Avoid publishing complete registration numbers unnecessarily when they could expose private individuals.
* Treat user-submitted images as material requiring source and provenance assessment.
* Corroborate plate identification with additional evidence before making strong claims.
