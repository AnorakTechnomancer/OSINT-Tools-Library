# Archiving OSINT

Find historical content, preserve pages you have already identified, or monitor future changes. File and document discovery tools are included separately in the quick picks because they do not provide historical website snapshots.

## Quick picks by task

- **Historical websites:** [Wayback Machine](../osint-tools/wayback-machine.md).
- **Archived social content:** [Arctic Shift](../osint-tools/arctic-shift.md) for Reddit, [4plebs](../osint-tools/4plebs.md) for selected 4chan boards, [Archivarix Tube Search](../osint-tools/archivarix-tube-search.md) for deleted YouTube metadata.
- **Capture and inspect a page:** [Lookyloo](../osint-tools/lookyloo.md).
- **Verifiable evidence capture:** [GetProofAnchor](../osint-tools/getproofanchor.md) online or [ProofSnap](../osint-tools/proofsnap.md) through a browser extension. Both distinguish paid capture from free verification.
- **Monitor changes:** [Follow That Page](../osint-tools/follow-that-page.md).
- **Find documents:** [Free Full PDF](../osint-tools/free-full-pdf.md), [Anna's Archive](../osint-tools/annas-archive.md).
- **Find shared files:** [4shared](../osint-tools/4shared.md), [SearchShared](../osint-tools/searchshared.md).
