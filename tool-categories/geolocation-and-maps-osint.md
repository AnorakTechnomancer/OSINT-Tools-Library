# Geolocation and Maps OSINT

Geolocation & Maps OSINT focuses on identifying locations and analysing geographic information using maps, imagery, environmental clues, coordinates, and location-linked data.

> **Choose faster:** Use **Access** to see whether a tool works directly online or requires local installation. The **Best for** column distinguishes general maps from specialist tools such as AI image geolocation, satellite imagery, street-level imagery, shadow analysis, and coordinate conversion.

## Quick picks by task

- **AI photo geolocation:** GeoSpy, Picarta, SPOT
- **Street-level visual verification:** Apple Maps, Baidu Maps, Mapillary
- **Satellite / Earth observation:** Copernicus Browser
- **Sun, moon and shadow analysis:** SunCalc, MoonCalc, ShadeMap, Shadowmap
- **Feature-based geolocation:** Bellingcat OpenStreetMap Search, GeoHints, Wikimapia
- **Coordinates:** EarthPoint Convert
- **Infrastructure:** Open Infrastructure Map, Surveillance under Surveillance
- **Location-linked media/events:** MW Geofind, Geoconfirmed, Monitor the Situation
- **Satellite tracking:** N2YO

## Cost legend

- **Free** — the core tool is usable without payment.
- **Freemium** — useful functionality is available without payment, while additional results, features, volume, or saved work require payment.
- **Paid** — meaningful use requires payment, credits, or a subscription.

> Pricing, rate limits, access methods, and account requirements change. Check the individual entry and its **Last verified** metadata as the migration progresses.
